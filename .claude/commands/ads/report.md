Generate a structured ad audit report from findings already discussed in this conversation, or from data provided by the user.

## Report Format

Produce a clean, client-ready audit report with the following sections:

---

# Ad Account Audit Report
**Date:** [today's date]
**Platforms audited:** [list]
**Prepared for:** $ARGUMENTS

---

## Executive Summary
2–3 sentence overview of account health and the single most important opportunity.

## Key Metrics Snapshot
Table of current performance vs. benchmarks for each platform (CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS, conversion rate, frequency).

## Critical Issues (High Priority)
For each: what it is, why it matters, exact fix with steps.

## Optimization Opportunities (Medium Priority)
For each: what it is, estimated impact, exact fix with steps.

## Quick Wins (Low Effort / Immediate)
Bulleted list of changes that take under 15 minutes to implement.

## 30-Day Action Plan
Week-by-week prioritized task list.

## Appendix
Any supporting data, calculations, or notes.

---

Use clear headings, tables where helpful, and concrete numbers throughout. Avoid vague recommendations — every suggestion should include the exact location in the platform UI where the change is made.
