## Google Ads Audit Checklist

### Search Campaigns
- Match type distribution — over-reliance on broad match without smart bidding data?
- Search terms report reviewed for irrelevant queries; negative keywords added
- Keyword duplication across ad groups causing cannibalization
- Ad strength ratings (Excellent/Good on RSAs)
- RSA asset performance — pinned headlines limiting optimization?

### Shopping / Performance Max
- Feed quality: missing GTINs, thin descriptions, low-quality images
- Product segmentation granular enough for bid control
- PMax asset group relevance to audience signals
- Search themes set appropriately
- Brand vs. non-brand traffic split in PMax

### Bidding
- Smart bidding strategy appropriate for conversion volume (need 30–50 conv/month minimum for Target CPA/ROAS)
- Portfolio bid strategies in use where appropriate
- Target CPA/ROAS set realistically vs. historical actuals

### Quality Score Components
- Expected CTR
- Ad relevance
- Landing page experience
- Low QS keywords (below 5) — pause or fix

### Conversions
- All key actions tracked (purchases, leads, calls, micro-conversions)
- Primary vs. secondary conversions configured correctly
- Enhanced conversions enabled
- Conversion windows match sales cycle

### Audience & Remarketing
- Remarketing lists applied (observation and targeting)
- Customer match lists uploaded and refreshed
- Similar segments applied to top campaigns
