Perform a comprehensive advertising account audit across all platforms provided by the user.

The user may provide account data, exported reports, screenshots, or describe their setup. If no data is provided, ask them which platforms they're running ads on and request relevant exports or metrics.

## Audit Checklist

### Tracking & Attribution
- Conversion tracking configured and firing correctly on all platforms
- Attribution windows appropriate for the sales cycle
- Cross-platform attribution conflicts (same conversions counted twice)
- UTM parameters consistent and complete

### Budget & Spend Efficiency
- Budget pacing (underspending or overspending)
- Budget allocation across campaigns relative to performance
- Dayparting — are ads running during high-converting hours?
- Impression share lost to budget vs. rank

### Audience & Targeting
- Audience overlap between ad sets/campaigns causing internal competition
- Remarketing audiences properly sized and segmented
- Exclusion lists in place (existing customers, low-intent audiences)
- Lookalike/similar audience quality

### Creative & Copy
- Ad fatigue — frequency too high on any audience
- Creative variety per ad set (3–5 variants recommended)
- Top and bottom performers — identify patterns
- Landing page relevance to ad copy

### Bidding & Optimization
- Bid strategy appropriate for campaign objective and data volume
- Conversion data sufficient to support automated bidding
- Target CPA/ROAS goals realistic relative to historical performance
- Quality Score / Relevance Score issues

### Structure
- Campaign and ad set structure logical and scalable
- Naming conventions consistent
- Duplicate or redundant campaigns
